# Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA) Modeling

PhosphogypsumBot incorporates a robust Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA) engine to evaluate the financial viability and long-term economic sustainability of phosphogypsum treatment pathways.

---

## ⚙️ Evaluation Paradigms

The `TEAEngine` models costs and revenues from two distinct perspectives:

### 1. Conventional Life Cycle Costing (CLCC)
CLCC assesses private, internal costs incurred by the plant operator over the project lifecycle, scaled to **1 tonne of treated phosphogypsum**:

$$\text{CLCC} = \text{CAPEX}_{\text{annualized, FU}} + \text{OPEX}_{\text{FU}} - \text{Revenue}_{\text{FU}}$$

### 2. Societal Life Cycle Costing (SLCC)
SLCC expands the cost boundary by internalizing external environmental damages (such as global warming, eutrophication, and human toxicity) using **shadow pricing**:

$$\text{SLCC} = \text{CLCC} + \text{External Cost}_{\text{FU}}$$

*Environmental externalities* are monetized based on characterization values derived from regional policy databases (e.g., carbon tax values, air pollution health damages) mapped in `pgloop/tea/external_cost.py`.

---

## 💵 Financial Cost Breakdown

### 1. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)
CAPEX includes initial purchases of reactors, filters, kilns, and piping infrastructure. The total capital expenditure is annualized over the plant lifetime using the capital recovery factor ($CRF$):

$$\text{CAPEX}_{\text{annualized}} = \text{CAPEX}_{\text{total}} \times \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$

Where:
*   $r$ is the discount rate (default: $5\%$).
*   $n$ is the plant operating lifetime (default: $20\text{ years}$).
*   The annualized CAPEX is divided by the annual throughput (tonnes/year) to obtain $\text{CAPEX}_{\text{annualized, FU}}$.

### 2. Operating Expenditure (OPEX)
OPEX represents day-to-day operational costs:
*   **Variable Cost**: Process consumables, chemical reagents, utility consumption (thermal heat duties, electricity).
*   **Fixed Cost**: Facility maintenance, operating labor, and overheads.

### 3. Revenue
Revenues are generated by selling high-value output products, such as:
*   $\alpha$-hemihydrate gypsum plaster
*   Precipitated calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$)
*   Recovered Rare Earth Elements (REEs)

## 📈 Long-Term Financial Indices

To assess whether a pathway is investment-ready, the engine computes three standard indices:

### Net Present Value (NPV)
Calculated as the sum of discounted cash flows over the project lifetime:

$$\text{NPV} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{\text{Net Cash Flow}_t}{(1+r)^t} - \text{CAPEX}_{\text{total}}$$

### Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
The discount rate at which the Net Present Value of all cash flows equals zero:

$$\text{NPV}(\text{IRR}) = 0$$

### Payback Period
The time (in years) required for cumulative net cash flows to recover the initial CAPEX investment.
